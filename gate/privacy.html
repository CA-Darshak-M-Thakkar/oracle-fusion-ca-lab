Privacy

This practice site is for a Chartered Accountancy class. It is not the real Oracle cloud.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we take only your name and email. We also note whether you are a Student or the Teacher. We do not ask for a phone number or an ICAI registration number. We do not ask you to type a password on this site.

Where it goes

That name and email are written to the teacher’s Google Sheet so the class register is marked. The sheet is used for attendance in this course. We do not sell this information. We do not share it with anyone else.
